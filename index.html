How to use the solver

  1. Choose your puzzle in the menu at the top: 2×2 to 15×15, Pyraminx, Skewb or Megaminx.
  2. Enter the scrambled state: paint the stickers on the net, scan every face with your camera or upload photos, or type the scramble in standard notation.
  3. Press Solve. The solver runs inside your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  4. Follow the solution: the 3D model turns step by step while the permutation graph shows every sticker moving. In Free play mode you can scramble and turn the puzzle yourself, with a timer.

What the permutation graph shows

Every sticker is a node and every move is a permutation of the stickers. The permutation splits into cycles, drawn as circles, so the graph shows the structure of the puzzle: which stickers can reach which places. A solution is a path through the puzzle's state graph from your scramble to the solved state, and the graph animates that path move by move.

Frequently asked questions

Which puzzles are supported?

Cubes from 2×2 to 15×15, the Pyraminx, the Skewb and the Megaminx. Each one has its own page with notation and measured move counts.

Is the solver free, and are my photos uploaded?

It is free and runs entirely in your browser. Camera images and photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies; Google Analytics is loaded only if you accept it in the banner, and you can change that choice in the footer.

Is the solution the shortest possible?

2×2, Pyraminx and Skewb solutions are proven optimal. 3×3 solutions are near-optimal (usually 19–21 moves). From 4×4 up the solvers use reduction methods, so solutions are longer but are found in seconds.

Can I scan my cube with the camera?

Yes, for cubes from 2×2 to 15×15: show each face to the camera or upload photos, then check the colors on the net. The Pyraminx, Skewb and Megaminx are entered by painting.
